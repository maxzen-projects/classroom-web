📊 BUSINESS LOGIC EXAMPLES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SCENARIO 1: MONTHLY BILL GENERATION
   Fee Structure: Tuition ₹5000, Transport ₹500, Due Day: 15
   ↓
   Bill Generated with:
   • Tuition: ₹5000
   • Previous Due: ₹0 (first month)
   • Extra Fees: ₹500
   • Total: ₹5500
   • Due Date: 15th of month
   • Status: Generated

SCENARIO 2: DUE CARRY FORWARD
   May Bill: Unpaid ₹5000
   June Bill Generated:
   • Tuition: ₹5000
   • Previous Due: ₹5000 (from May)
   • Total: ₹10000
   • isCarriedForward: true
   • carriedFromBillId: <May Bill ID>

SCENARIO 3: LATE FEE APPLICATION
   Due Date: May 15
   Check Date: May 20 (5 days late)
   Late Fee: ₹200 (fixed)
   ↓
   Bill Updated:
   • Late Fee: ₹200
   • Total Amount: +₹200
   • Status: Overdue
   • isLateFeesApplied: true

SCENARIO 4: PAYMENT RECORDING
   Bill Due Amount: ₹2000
   Payment: ₹1200
   ↓
   Result:
   • Paid Amount: ₹1200
   • Due Amount: ₹800
   • Status: Partially Paid
   • Payment Record: Created with receipt
